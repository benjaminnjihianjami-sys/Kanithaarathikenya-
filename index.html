The Covenant — IrĩkanĩroIrĩkanĩro cia Ngai na Kanitha

Eleven sacred points of covenant revealed to the founders — the foundation upon which Kanitha wa Arathi Kenya stands. Supported by: Joel 1:1-8 · Ezekiel 33:1 · Ezekiel 37:1 · Kiambiriria 34:1 · Jeremiah 23:1

Irĩkanĩro ikumi na ĩmwe cia mũtheru ĩheetwo andũ a mbere — ĩthĩĩra rĩa Kanitha wa Arathi Kenya. Joel 1:1-8 · Ezekiel 33:1 · Ezekiel 37:1 · Jeremiah 23:1

  • I Intercede for the lost children of Israel (the nations) Kũrĩrĩra Israel tondũ wa maũndũ maria mahetwo ni ageni Pray for the nations in suffering Mathina ma mataĩfa
  • II Separate from worldly customs, as God has rejected them Kwamũkana na andũ a ĩrore — tondũ marĩ endĩa aa rũrĩrĩ na ĩthaka
  • III Replace the high place (shrine) of Nyakerũ with prayer Kũhingwo gũthiĩ thĩbĩtarĩ niũndũ wa ũhinga ũria warĩ wa Nyakerũ
  • IV Restore the possessions and heritage of the elders Kũgĩrĩo ĩndo cia ageni na maũndũ mao — Ngai akiuga Kanitha ũcokerere maũndũ ma maithe mao
  • V Provide for the nations and their spiritual leaders Kũruma na gũthahia maũndũ ma ageni na maorutani mao
  • VI Establish a council (Thirikari) — God appoints before the Arathi Kũhoera kiama (Thirikari) — Ngai agĩathana kiama nĩ Arathi a mbere
  • VII Seek the gift of education for the people Kũhoya tondũ wa githomo (Education)
  • VIII Pray for Israel — for sufferings that have become a way of life Kũhoya na kũrĩrĩra Israel tondũ wa mathina maria marĩ namo ma ũndũĩre
  • IX Abstain from flesh and blood — for blood is life Kũhingwo kũria nyama hamwe na thakame tondũ thakame niyo muoyo
  • X Pray with hands raised upward to God Kũhoya tuoyete moko matheru na ĩgũrũ
  • XI Seek the fruits of the field — things that grow in the earth Kũhoya tondũ wa mĩtamaiyu ya mũgũnda

JULY 1930

The Church Founded — Gwitwo Nĩ Ngai

Kanitha Ĩambĩrĩĩra — Gwitwo Nĩ Ngai

Nine people are called by God on Mount Kenya to form Kanitha wa Arathi Kenya. Erasto Wariĩ is sent by the Holy Spirit from Kirinyaga to preach the covenant with God.

Andũ kenda metĩrwo nĩ Ngai thĩĩni wa Kirinyaga kũhanda Kanitha wa Arathi Kenya. Erasto Wariĩ waheetwo nĩ Roho Mũtheru ũria rũgendo rũgũthio.

FoundationMũthĩĩnia
4 AUGUST 1930

Mission Journey Begins — Nyeri

Rũgendo Rũambĩrĩĩra — Nyeri

Titus Gitanga, Henry Maina, Jason Kanini and Elijah Gichuhi set out. They travel to Nyeri, then split — Titus and Elijah to Kamũgoiri, Henry and Jason to Ngithi.

Titus Gitanga, Henry Maina, Jason Kanini na Elijah Gichuhi maathiĩ rũgendo. Titus na Elijah kũrĩ Kamũgoiri, Henry na Jason kũrĩ Ngithi.

MissionRũgendo
16 AUGUST 1930

The Holy Spirit Descends

Roho Mũtheru Agũka — Kirĩkanĩro

The Holy Spirit moves powerfully. Those who had refused to pray are moved. This marks the true spiritual birth of the Church — Roho amaikũrũkira, and the church formally takes shape.

Roho amaikũrũkira, tondũ nĩwatonyire kũrĩ o athe matĩacokire kũhinga maitho, ũgĩtuika Kanitha wa Arathi.

Spiritual EventŨhoro wa Roho
17 AUGUST 1930

First Service at Babeli

Kirĩkanĩro Kia Mbere — Babeli

First gathering at Babeli at 3pm (12pm by their reckoning). They sing hymns, read scriptures — Psalms 115, Hymn 54 — fulfilling what was spoken. The congregation prays and worships together for the first time.

Kirĩkanĩro kia mbere hau Babeli thaa thita (12pm). Maakinya kirima kia Babeli, mambiriria kũhoya, makinya kirima kia Babeli na maakĩhoya makĩthiũrũrũkĩrio.

First WorshipKũhoya Kwa Mbere
14 AUGUST 1930

Embu — New Converts Welcomed

Embu — Andũ Mũgwanja Amũkĩrwo

At Embu, seven new believers are welcomed: John Muhira, Michelle Mwenyika, Misheck Thaara, Erastus, Ayub Philip, Agendu Joel and Philip Mwalumbwa — all received into the fellowship.

Hau Embu, andũ mũgwanja amũkĩrwo — John Muhira, Michelle Mwenyika, Misheck Thaara, Erastus, Ayub Philip, Agendu Joel na Philip Mwalumbwa.

New MembersAndũ Mũgwanja
22–25 AUGUST 1930

Great Worship at Babeli — Scriptures & Songs

Kũhoya Kwa Nene — Babeli

They gather in the church, sing from Psalms 198 and 108, and read from scriptures of Mwathani: Psalms 90:1, 131:1, 55:1, 16:1, 86:1, 102:1 and more. On 25/8 they proceed to Ngithi.

Mweri 22/8/1930 tũtanakinya kirima mũgongo, tũgĩkoma ngurunga ya mitomo ina. Mweri 24/8/1930 tũgĩthiĩ kirima mugongo na kwarĩ Mũthenya wa kiumia.

Scripture & WorshipŨhoro na Rwĩmbo
27 AUG – 9 SEP 1930

Chogoria, Kamũgoiri & Giitwa

Rũgendo Rũgĩthia — Chogoria na Giitwa

They visit Timothy Mtu Imwethi at Chogoria, Philip Mkubwa on 28/8, then arrive at Kamũgoiri on 8/9 and at Giitwa on 9/9 — the Spirit moving at each place.

Mweri 27/8/1930 twakinyire kwa Timotheo Mtu Imwethi Chogoria. Mweri 8/9 twakinyire kamũgoiri, na mweri 9/9 twahunjirie Giitwa.

Mission ContinuesRũgendo Rũgĩthia
20 SEPTEMBER 1930

Mission Ends — Return to Muranga

Rũgendo Rũgĩthia — Gũcoka Mũrang'a

On Saturday 20/9/1930 they give thanks and take leave of Jacob Mũchiri's homestead. The journey concludes with twelve people gathered — the foundation fully laid.

Mweri 20/9/1930 (Saturday) niguo twoimire kũhoya tondũ wakinyitwo nĩ thabari ya Jacob Mũchiri. Tũgĩtũmwo Mũrang'a tũrĩ andũ ikũmi na erĩ.

19 JULY 1964

Nairobi Conference — 34 Years Later

Kirĩkanĩro kĩa Nairobi — 1964

Kenya African United Kristian Church conference in Nairobi. Leaders from across Kenya — Kikuyu, Kipsigis, Luo, Luhya — gather. Chaired by Gideon Omul with G.C. Henry Maina signing. Erusto Warii among delegates.

Kirĩkanĩro kĩa Kanitha — Nairobi. Athuri a kanitha kũuma Kenya yothe maahuĩkana. Mwene Gĩti Mũnene Gideon Omul, G.C. Henry Maina akĩandĩka. Erusto Warii arĩ hĩĩ.
